# Webstore design

`WebstoreCategory` maps Discord choice values to fixed official endpoint names, labels and observed category selectors. `PointBlankClient.getWebstoreHtml` constructs URLs with URLSearchParams, validates HTML/access responses and participates in the same gate/retry policy as clan/player retrieval and logo navigation. Redirects are restricted to supported public paths on the same HTTPS origin.

Public catalogue documents contain uninvoked `goPayletter`, `goWishList`, `goCart`, `goHistory` and `goLogin` function declarations with login alerts/redirects for shopping actions. A catalogue-specific access check removes only their exact observed declaration bodies from inspection, without executing any scripts. Any remaining redirect/login marker, invocation of those actions in scripts or page-load attributes, CAPTCHA, 401/403 or off-endpoint redirect still fails clearly. Other endpoint access guards retain their existing behavior.

The pure parser scopes products to `<category>.goods > .inner > .item_wrap > .item_list` and reads direct `li` children, excluding nested account-limited-sale rows. Each product requires `.con > .desc > .name` and one `.con > a.s_link` linking to the official `/webstore/goods/view` with its own numeric `productIdx`. Product queries are preserved rather than reconstructed. The image is `.con > .item > .img > img`, allowed only from the configured official HTTPS host or the observed `cdn2.pointblank.id` CDN. Untrusted images are omitted; untrusted required product links fail the page.

Prices come from `.con > .desc > .price > font`; original prices come from `.del_line`. Known same-origin `coin_pb.png` and `coin_pb02.png` identify PB Cash, `coin_gold.png` identifies Golden Ticket, and `/images/common/webshop/token/star_ticket/img_token_star_ticket.png` identifies Star Ticket. Other icons remain unknown. Discounts use `.con > .item > .sale > font` percent text; ratings use direct `.rate` numeric text in the range 0–5. Missing/malformed numeric data stays null. Product tags come only from `.con > .desc > .state > span`.

Pagination is `<category>.goods > .inner > .paging_wrap > ul`. Its single `.on` row must match the requested page. Observed links use `javascript:goPage(integer);`; disabled links are `javascript:void(0);`. The parser extracts only integers without executing JavaScript. `.last` gives the final page, or the current page when disabled; `.next` and `.prev` must agree with adjacent pages and final/first boundaries. Missing or inconsistent required markup raises UNEXPECTED_HTML rather than silently returning an empty list or duplicating page one.

`WebstoreService` holds a bounded `AsyncCache<WebstorePage>` keyed by validated category URL/page. Loading, parsing and normalization occur under one deduplicated promise; rejected loads are removed. Cached snapshots retain their fetch timestamp. The default TTL is two minutes.

`webstorePage` builds at most five bounded EmbedBuilders with original thumbnails, escaped names/tags, disabled mentions and product URLs. The reply header links to the source catalogue page and shows the upstream page/count plus the visible item range. The final card carries source/fetch timestamp and expiry status. Null fields display N/A.

`executeWebstore` maintains a current upstream page and zero-based five-card view. Local movement needs no HTTP; crossing a page boundary calls the service and selects that page's first/last view. A message-scoped collector acknowledges immediately, serializes navigation and commits state after the response edit succeeds. Retrieval failure sends a private readable follow-up and leaves state untouched. Collector expiry/shutdown removes active IDs and disables controls after pending edits drain. An expiry flag also disables any response completed during an in-flight request. The global interaction handler handles stale controls without competing with active collectors. Explicit deployment includes `/webstore` alongside the existing three commands.
